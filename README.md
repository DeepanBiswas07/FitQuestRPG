## 🌲 The Skill Tree (100+ Masteries)

Map out your training across **6 progressive branches** spanning **5 Tiers of Difficulty** (from Initiate Tier I to Legend Tier V):

1.  **Push**: Tame vertical and horizontal pushing, progressing to the legendary One-Arm Push-up.
2.  **Pull**: Scale vertical cliffs by mastering rows, pull-ups, and the ultimate Front Lever.
3.  **Core**: Build a core of solid iron with L-Sits, Dragon Flags, and Ab Wheel rollouts.
4.  **Legs**: Develop explosive lower body power from standard squats up to Pistol and Shrimp squats.
5.  **Wall**: Master handstands, handstand pushups, and vertical wall walks.
6.  **Mobility**: Unlock your range of motion, thaw frozen joints, and prepare your body for splits.

### ⚡ Glowing Pathing
Unlock pre-requisite skills to see energy lines flow on your tree:
*   **Sapphire Flow 🌊**: Unlocked and active paths ready for you to train.
*   **Emerald Glow 🌲**: Fully mastered branches showing your completed progress.

### ⚔️ Combat Mechanics
*   **Hit Splashes & Screenshakes**: Completing a set inflicts physical damage to the boss, triggering a structural screen shake and releasing a burst of **15 glowing spark particles**.
*   **Quest Lore**: Procedural quest descriptions are generated based on the workout types you choose, telling the story of your battle.

## 💎 Battle Runes & Modifiers

Socket modifiers into your exercises to customize your training pace and boost rewards:
*   **⏱️ Tempo Rune**: Perform slow, controlled negative movements. *(Earns +50% XP)*
*   **💥 Plyo Rune**: Execute explosive fast concentric movements. *(Earns +30% XP)*
*   **🎒 Weight Rune**: Add household resistance (backpack, water jugs). *(Earns +100% XP)*
